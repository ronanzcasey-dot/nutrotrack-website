Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Nutro. Can't find your answer? Email support.

Getting started
What is Nutro?+

Nutro is a calorie and macro tracker for iPhone. Log meals by searching a food database, scanning a barcode, or describing a meal to the AI estimator, then track calories, protein, carbs, and fat against your daily goals, with trends and streaks to keep you on track.

Is Nutro free?+

Nutro and all of its features are completely free to download and use, and shows ads to support the free version. An optional ad-free subscription is available if you'd prefer no ads.

Do I need to create an account?+

No, most features work without signing in. Creating an account (with Sign in with Apple, Google, or email) is optional and only needed if you want your logs synced across devices via iCloud.

What devices does Nutro support?+

Nutro is built for iPhone (iOS). There isn't an Android or web version currently.

Logging food
How does the AI food estimator work?+

Describe a meal in plain language (or snap a photo) and Nutro sends that description to an AI provider, which returns an estimated calorie and macro breakdown. It's a fast estimate, not a lab measurement, so results can vary from the exact nutrition facts, especially for home-cooked or mixed dishes.

Why does the AI feature sometimes feel slow or occasionally fail?+

AI responses are generated by a third-party AI provider, and requests can briefly queue or fail during periods of high demand on their end. If an AI request fails, just try again in a moment. This doesn't affect manual logging, food search, or barcode scanning, which don't depend on the AI provider.

How do I scan a barcode?+

Tap the barcode icon when adding a food and point your camera at the product barcode. Nutro looks up the product in a nutrition database and fills in the details automatically.

Can I add my own custom foods or recipes?+

Yes, you can create custom foods with your own nutrition values so they're ready to log again later.

Data & privacy
Where is my data stored?+

Your food logs, photos, weight, and goals are stored on your device and, if you sign in, synced to your personal iCloud account, not to servers we operate. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does Nutro share my data with AI providers?+

Only the content of a specific AI request (e.g. a food description, photo, or question) is sent to the AI provider handling that request, in order to generate a response. It isn't used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the current list of providers.

Can I sync with Apple Health?+

Yes, with your permission Nutro can read and write data like weight and dietary energy to Apple HealthKit. This is used only to power features you request, not for advertising.

How do I delete my data?+

You can delete individual entries in the app, or delete the app itself along with its iCloud data via iOS Settings to remove everything. For account deletion help, contact nutrotrack@gmail.com.

Subscription & support
How do I cancel my subscription?+

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select Nutro, and cancel there.

I found a bug or have feedback, who do I contact?+

Email nutrotrack@gmail.com with what happened, and a screenshot if you have one. That's the fastest way to reach support.
